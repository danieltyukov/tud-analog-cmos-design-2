BOOSTER TOPOLOGY (Binkley Fig.7 template; both are differential folded cascodes + ref-pinned CMFB)
  BA  NMOS-cascode booster  (in-signal-path)  : PMOS input pair, folds DOWN, drives Vnl/Vnr (gates
       of Mn8.2/Mn6.2). Senses the NMOS cascode SOURCE nodes Vgnp/Vgnn (CM ~0.18 V, low -> PMOS input).
  BB  PMOS-cascode booster  (off-path)        : NMOS input pair, folds UP, drives Vpr/Vpl (gates of
       Mp2.2/Mp4.2). Senses PMOS cascode SOURCE nodes Vgpn/Vgpp (CM ~1.55 V, high -> NMOS input).
  Each booster output common-mode is pinned to the cascode-gate bias (Vcn for BA, Vcp for BB) by a
  small reference-pinned CMFB (NMOS diff pair vs Vcn/Vcp + PMOS mirror, drives the output pull device).
  Bias reuses existing rails: Vbp/Vbn (tail+source currents), Vcn/Vcp (booster cascode gates).
  CM-sense: 100 M� resistors average the two outputs -> Vcmoa/Vcmob -> CMFB diff-pair input.

  >>> WHY ref-pinned CMFB (not self-biased): a self-biased CMFB lands the cascode-gate CM where the
  pull device happens to source the branch current. For BB that landed Vpr at 566 mV (vs Vcp 873),
  which pulled the PMOS cascode source to 1365 mV and BALLOONED the main-amp branch current
  177->256 uA -> I(Vdd) 1.768 mA (BREACH). Pinning the gate CM to Vcn/Vcp restores the cascode
  sources to ~ideal (Vgnp 172 mV, Vgpn 1553 mV) and the main amp to its 1.547 mA bias. <<<

BOOSTER LOOP / DOUBLET
  BA local boost gain  Vnl/Vgnp = 120 (41.6 dB); holds the cascode source to ~0.02 % of the output swing.
  Booster UGF ~ gm_in/(2.pi.C_gate) ~ 0.5 GHz (gm_in ~ 97 uS, C_gate ~ 30 fF) -> FASTER than the Binkley
  0.8.w_u(211 MHz)/0.4.w_u(106 MHz) targets. Faster is conservative here: it pushes the booster pole-zero
  DOUBLET well BEYOND w_u (52.9 MHz loop crossover / 264 MHz GBW), so it does not create a slow settling
  tail. Confirmed by the clean single-pole AC (PM 85.9, no peaking) and the monotone CM recovery.
  Full high-accuracy settling closure (T38.6-T30, 0.05% in 25 ns) is Phase 5.

POWER-CEILING WATCH (carry-forward)
  Pre-Phase-4: 1.547 mA. Post-Phase-4: 1.658 mA (+111 uA boosters) = 97.5 % of the 1.7 mA / 3 mW ceiling.
  42 uA headroom remains. Current-reuse (Phase 5) halves the input-branch current (the dominant term)
  and creates ample margin; it is in any case mandatory for FoM.
