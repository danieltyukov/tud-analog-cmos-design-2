## Measurement and reporting

1. **Power.** Use the total supply current of the whole amplifier: stage 1, stage 2 and CMFB, all of
   them; the bias network is excluded. Using the average current is reasonable. Our rule
   (1.8 V × average I(Vdd), Vdd feeding every amplifier block, bias block on Vddb) is accepted. State
   which blocks are on Vdd in the report.
2. **SNR must include kT/C.** $\mathrm{SNR} = V_{sig,rms}^2 / (v_{n,kTC}^2 + v_{n,amp}^2)$, with the kT/C of
   both halves (differential). `tools/metrics.py` already uses $42\,kT/C_{in}$ (21 per side); the report must
   show the computation explicitly (signal, kT/C, amplifier noise, total) so a reader sees it.
3. **FoM plausibility.** In 5 to 10 years of this course the best designs reach about 176 dB, at most
   177 dB. Higher means something is wrong. (Attempt 2 claimed 183 dB; its τ was taken from a
   non-exponential large-signal recovery: τ 1.64 ns while its loop had fc 39.8 MHz, 1/ωc = 4.0 ns.)
4. **Settling curve.** It must look like ordinary two-stage settling: slewing (if any), then an
   exponential approach (a straight line in dB versus time). The graders inspect it; a strange curve
   is likely a fail. The FoM comparison (D6) must use the same kT/C-inclusive SNR for both designs.
5. **Pages.** 8 pages is the target; one page more does not matter much.
6. Questions go by e-mail, not Teams.

## Design

7. **Architecture.** The class-AB, current-reuse design is too complicated. Recommended: a simple two-stage
   amplifier, **telescopic cascode stage 1 and a common-source stage 2**; about 90 % of passing students
   use this.
8. If a class-AB output is used anyway, bias MN2 and MP2 with **two separate RC level shifters**, not one.
   In attempt 2 MN2 sat almost in cutoff (subthreshold).
9. **Bias network: use a replica bias.** The bias block should mirror the amplifier's own transistor
   arrangement (same stacking, same device types and lengths, scaled down), so each bias voltage is
   generated by a scaled copy of the device it biases. Mirroring fixed multiples of one reference current
   into unrelated transistors is "not a smart way".
